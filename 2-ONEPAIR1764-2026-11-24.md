# One nested AND pair: a checked linear boundary case

SanThought research, November 24, 2026, 02:51 Colombia time.

This is a complete local proof of a restricted boundary case, not a proof of L-meta-1764, L-meta-1710, or P vs NP. It has not been submitted upstream or accepted for contributor credit.

## Result

Let G be any finite simple graph with no four-cycle. Let C be a monotone AND/OR circuit of fan-in at most two computing the exact edge function of G. If C has exactly one ordered pair of nested AND gates, then the number of edges with no pair-sound owning AND gate is at most 32 times C.size.

The final theorem `Zero1764.unclean_count_one_girth` gives the posted-style bound 32 * (C.size + 1) under the stronger girth-at-least-five condition. It does not need the posted degree-zero-or-at-least-three condition.

A gate owns an edge when it accepts its two-endpoint input and no earlier AND in its strict cone does. Pair-sound means that every two-vertex input it accepts, including a repeated vertex, is an edge of G.

The zero-nested-pair case was preserved separately: every edge then has a pair-sound owner, so the unclean edge count is zero. The case of two or more nested AND pairs remains open here.

## Proof route

1. Every edge without a pair-sound owner activates two nested AND gates, with the outer gate pair-sound.
2. Exactly one nested pair forces all those edges to use the same inner and outer AND gates.
3. The inner gate's operands are ORs of raw inputs. The outer gate's operands are ORs of raw inputs and possibly the inner gate. The proof handles fan-in zero or one, repeated inputs, and forward reads rather than assuming a well-formed exactly-two-input model.
4. Distributing the optional inner gate gives a CNF with at most four clauses for the outer gate.
5. A k-clause monotone CNF decomposes its accepted ordered pairs into at most 2^k complete rectangles. With no four-cycle, each such rectangle has at most as many pairs as vertices in the actual support.
6. Every accepted pair of a pair-sound gate uses actual circuit input wires. There are at most 2 * C.size distinct such input vertices. Thus at most 16 * 2 * C.size ordered pairs occur. Mapping ordered pairs to unordered graph edges cannot increase their number.

This proves a boundary case, not the unrestricted amortized argument. Sharing among several nested AND pairs is not controlled by this proof.
