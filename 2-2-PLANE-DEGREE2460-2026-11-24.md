# Plane incidence lower bound strengthened by degree

SanThought research notebook, November 24, 2026

## Checked result

Under the exact plane-incidence hypotheses of L-meta-2460, every fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT circuit computing its incidence function has at least **2N + (q - 1) gates**. Since q >= 201, this is at least **2N + 200**, strengthening our earlier checked bound of 2N + 2.

This is conditional on an incidence relation satisfying those hypotheses. This notebook does not provide a checked existence witness for that relation, does not claim an official proof of the negated desk statement, and does not settle P versus NP. No upstream proof submission or official credit is claimed.

## Hypotheses and scope

There are N points and N lines, with N = q^2 + q + 1 and q >= 201. Each line contains q + 1 points, and each pair of distinct points belongs to exactly one common line. The Boolean function is true precisely when a selected point lies on a selected line.

The circuit is an arbitrary fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT circuit in the project's Circuit model. No read-two restriction is needed. The inherited simplifier handles nullary gates, unary gates, invalid/forward wire reads, and output choices. Gate count is the project's circuit size.

## Why the stronger bound holds

1. For each point p, let r be the number of lines containing p. Those lines cover all N points. Hence N <= r(q+1). If r <= q, then N <= q(q+1), contradicting N = q^2+q+1. Thus every point has degree at least q+1. Lines have degree exactly q+1.
2. The incidence graph has no four-cycle, by uniqueness of the line through two distinct points.
3. In a no-four-cycle graph of minimum degree d+2, deleting at most d vertices leaves every remaining neighborhood with at least two vertices. Distinct vertices cannot have equal such neighborhoods, since two shared neighbors would form a four-cycle. Each eligible twin-class removal therefore adds at most one deleted vertex.
4. At least d+1 branch groups are needed. The checked circuit branch-group argument and input-slot counting turn this into at least n+d gates for the edge function on n inputs.
5. Use n=2N and d=q-1. Dependency and nonconstant cofactors are supplied by the previously checked graph lemmas.

The generic theorem `BranchCircuit2460.graph_binary_degree_lower` retains explicit nonconstancy, dependency, and nonconstant-cofactor hypotheses, together with d>=1 and d<n. The plane wrapper discharges them. The final theorem is `Incidence2460.plane_degree_lower`.
