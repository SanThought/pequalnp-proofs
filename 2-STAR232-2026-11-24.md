# A maximal two-dimensional symmetric permanent level space

SanThought research notebook, November 24, 2026

## Checked results

We constructed a **two-dimensional complex linear space** of symmetric 4 x 4 matrices t satisfying **perm(J+t)=24**, where J is the all-ones matrix. We also proved this particular space is **maximal under inclusion**: no larger symmetric constant-permanent linear space can contain it.

This does not prove that every such space has dimension at most two. In particular, it does not settle L-gct-232, which asks whether a three-dimensional space can exist, or its exact twin L-gct-231. A different three-dimensional space not containing our star plane has not been excluded. No upstream submission or official credit is claimed. This does not settle P versus NP.

## Explicit plane

For arbitrary a,b in the complex numbers, set

```
t = [ 0   a   b  -a-b ]
    [ a   0   0    0  ]
    [ b   0   0    0  ]
    [-a-b 0   0    0  ]
```

The map (a,b) to t is complex-linear and injective. The range has finrank two, is symmetric, and every member satisfies perm(J+t)=24. All of these statements, including dimension and the full permanent identity, are checked in Lean.

## Maximality proof

Suppose a symmetric direction M is compatible with the entire plane: perm(J+star(a,b)+zM)=24 for all complex a,b,z. Expand the 4 x 4 permanent as its 24 permutation products.

1. Extract the coefficient of z from evaluations at z=1,-1,2,-2. Six choices of (a,b) give linear equations on M.
2. Those equations force M into a normal form. Its lower 3 x 3 block is a single value d, its first row off the diagonal is (u,v,w), and M00=-9d-2(u+v+w).
3. In that normal form, coefficient equations from perm(J+zM)=24 force d cubed to be zero. Over the complex numbers d=0. The quadratic coefficient then gives (u+v+w) squared equal to zero, hence u+v+w=0.
4. Thus M itself lies in the original star plane. Applying this to every member of a containing subspace proves equality with the plane.

The key final theorems are `Gct232.two_dimensional_boundary` and `Gct232.star_maximal`. Intermediate checked lemmas include `permanent_four`, `compatible_derivative`, `compatible_normal_form`, `reduced_no_extension`, and `compatible_direction`.
