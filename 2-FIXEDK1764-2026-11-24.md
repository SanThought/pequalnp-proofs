# Unclean-edge bounds for every fixed nesting-pair budget

SanThought research notebook, November 24, 2026

## Checked result

The earlier zero-, one-, and two-pair work now extends to **every fixed natural number K**. For a monotone fan-in-two AND/OR circuit C computing a graph edge function, assume the graph has no four-cycle and `andPairs C <= K`. Then the number of edges without a pair-sound owner is at most

**K * 2^(B(K,K)+1) * size(C)**,

where B(K,0)=2 and B(K,d+1)=2*B(K,d)^K.

Here `andPairs` counts strict ancestor-descendant pairs of AND gates, not all pairs of AND gates. The exact definitions of owners, cones, and pair-soundness are the project's definitions.

**This is not the full L-meta-1764 statement.** That statement asks for some single universal constant with no bound on nesting pairs. Our constant depends on K and grows rapidly. Choosing K from each individual circuit does not make the constant uniform. No upstream submission or official credit is claimed. This does not settle P versus NP.

## Proof structure

1. If at most K earlier AND atoms feed an operand and each atom has a CNF with at most B clauses, distributing their OR gives at most B^K clauses. Raw input variables are absorbed into every clause.
2. Combining at most two such operands gives at most 2*B^K clauses for an AND gate. Iterating gives the recurrence above.
3. An AND gate has at most K strict AND ancestors when the total nesting-pair count is at most K. A strict ancestor loses at least one member of this ancestor set, so AND nesting depth is at most K. The total number of nested outer AND gates is also at most K.
4. A pair-sound gate represented by at most L CNF clauses accepts at most 2^(L+1)*size(C) ordered vertex pairs in a no-four-cycle graph. The proof decomposes accepted pairs into at most 2^L rectangles; each sound rectangle is thin. Used-input support has size at most twice the gate count.
5. Every edge lacking a pair-sound owner is covered by a pair-sound nested outer AND gate. Summing over at most K such gates gives the displayed bound.

The final theorem is `Zero1764.unclean_count_fixed_pairs`. Intermediate results include `bounded_depth_CNF`, `and_ancestor_depth`, `pair_count_from_CNF`, and `unclean_count_bounded_depth`. A more general wrapper allows separate ancestor, depth, and outer-gate bounds.

This general bound is much weaker numerically than the earlier specialized one- and two-pair results. The earlier checked bounds remain useful and unchanged: one pair gives 32*size, two pairs gives 1024*size.

## Additional checked graph refinement

The archive also preserves `EdgeDegreeSharp.lean`. It strengthens the previously published degree-sensitive circuit bound by one gate: a no-four-cycle graph on n vertices with minimum degree delta >= 3 and delta-1 < n has edge-function fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT circuit size at least **n+delta-1**. Dependency and nonconstant cofactors are derived, not extra assumptions in its final wrapper.

Consequently, the exact L-meta-2460 plane hypotheses imply at least **2N+q gates**. This is a follow-on strengthening of our valid earlier 2N+q-1 bound, not a correction. It remains conditional on a plane-incidence relation satisfying those hypotheses; no checked existence witness or full negation of the desk statement is claimed.
