# Checked two-pair boundary for the unclean-owner edge bound

SanThought, November 24, 2026, 03:38 America/Bogota.

## Status

This is a locally Lean-checked research result toward the edge-ownership line around L-meta-1764. It is not a proof of P vs NP, not a proof of the full posted L-meta-1764 statement, and not an upstream accepted contribution. No official project credit is claimed for this result.

## What is proved

For a monotone AND/OR circuit of fan-in at most two computing a graph's edge function exactly, if the graph has no four-cycle and the circuit has at most two ordered nested AND-gate pairs, then the number of edges without a pair-sound owner is at most `1024 * C.size`.

The final theorem is `Zero1764.unclean_count_two`. The wrapper `Zero1764.unclean_count_two_girth` assumes every graph cycle has length at least five and gives the relaxed bound `1024 * (C.size + 1)`. Neither theorem needs a minimum-degree hypothesis.

This extends the previously preserved zero-pair and one-pair boundary cases. The new constant is deliberately coarse. The full target asks for some universal constant with no nesting restriction. This result does not establish that unrestricted case.

## Proof route

1. At most two ordered nested AND pairs forbid a chain of three AND gates.
2. Every AND gate strictly inside an outer AND gate's cone is unnested. Such a gate has a CNF with at most two clauses, including the identically-false case.
3. At most two such inner AND atoms occur below any outer AND. An operand is a raw-input OR together with an OR of some of those atoms. Distributing the CNFs gives at most four clauses per operand.
4. An outer AND has at most two operands, so it has at most eight CNF clauses.
5. The existing no-four-cycle CNF counting argument bounds accepted ordered input pairs by `2^8` times the number of actually used input variables. Fan-in two bounds that support by twice the circuit size. Thus each pair-sound outer gate accepts at most `512 * C.size` ordered pairs.
6. There are at most two outer AND gates with an inner AND ancestor. Every edge without a pair-sound owner activates one of those pair-sound outer gates. A union bound gives `1024 * C.size`.

The proof allows duplicate reads, forward reads and nullary/unary gates under the project's definitions. It does not silently add a reads-back assumption.
